Last Vertex: Idle Defense

Idle tower defense · Android

You are the last vertex, one glowing triangle at the bottom of a retro CRT screen, and everything above it wants through. Upgrade, summon tattoos, fuse them, and survive 30 tiers of endless waves. Play actively or let it run while you are away.

English, Türkçe, Español and Português.

About

Lunemoth Games is a one-person studio. Our games are built to be easy to pick up, respectful of your time, and honest about how they work: the odds of every random reward are shown on screen, there are no forced ads, and your progress stays on your device unless you choose to back it up.
